Privacy

PL Draft · last updated 8 November 2026

PL Draft is a free hobby game. It has no ads and nothing is sold or shared.

What we store

  • If you play as a guest: nothing that identifies you. A random guest ID is used while you play, and it is deleted automatically after 14 days without activity. Guest results are never saved in the Hall of Fame or statistics.
  • If you sign in with Google: your Google account ID and email (used only to sign you in), the manager name you choose, your full name (only visible to you and the game admin, never to other players), an optional profile picture you upload, and your game results, records and statistics.

What we don't do

  • We never post anything to your Google account and never read your contacts, files or anything else.
  • We don't use tracking or advertising cookies.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with Supabase (database and sign-in), and the app is hosted on GitHub Pages.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time from your profile with "Delete account". This removes your profile, picture, records and statistics permanently.

Contact

Questions: contact the game admin (the person who invited you), or use the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen.
