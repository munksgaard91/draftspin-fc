Privacy

PL Draft · last updated 8 November 2026

PL Draft is a free hobby game. It has no ads and nothing is sold or shared.

What we store

  • If you play as a guest: nothing that identifies you. A random guest ID is used while you play, and it is deleted automatically after 14 days without activity. Guest results are never saved in the Hall of Fame or statistics.
  • If you sign in with Google: your Google account ID and email (used only to sign you in), the manager name you choose, your full name,an optional profile picture you upload, and your game results, records and statistics.

What we don't do

  • We never post anything to your Google account and never read your contacts, files or anything else.
  • We don't use tracking or advertising cookies.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with Supabase (database and sign-in), and the app is hosted on GitHub Pages.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time from your profile with "Delete account". This removes your profile, picture, records and statistics permanently and instantly.

Names, logos and images

PL Draft is an unofficial, non-commercial fan project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to the Premier League, its clubs or its players. Club names, logos, player names and images belong to their respective owners and are used for non-commercial fan purposes only.

The game is and will always be free. It is never sold, has no ads or in-app purchases, and is never used for marketing or any other kind of income. If you own any of this material and want it removed, contact the game admin and it will be taken down.

Contact

Questions: contact the game admin, or use the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen.
